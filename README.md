# MARS Team Creepers Design

This is the official repository for the MARS Rocket design for Team Creeper. 

## AV Bay Design
This is the Avionics Bay, designed in Blender and tested via various CAD softwares. 

<img src="https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/1558150795923300362/1568055758426341476/avbayrender1.png?ex=6af06779&is=6aef15f9&hm=bc496c9111542c39b15a92988ff5c30dc0d1b1343d72f046162e0bb58039a76e&" alt="AVBay" width="500">

The actual avionics consist of a custom PCB to collect flight data, and a 3.7V LiPo 503450 battery.  

## Rocket Design
The rocket was generally tested and modelled in OpenRocket. Details to come soon.
